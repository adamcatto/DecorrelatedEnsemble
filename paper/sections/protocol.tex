\section{Experimental Protocol}
All initial settings are fixed before each run. Outer folds remain unused during
candidate screening and selection; selected specifications are refitted on the
full outer training fold. Any future tuning regenerates the complete pipeline
inside an additional tuning split, rather than slicing a globally fitted OOF
matrix. All baselines share outer splits and fold-local preprocessing.
Development synthetic regimes are separate from any locked real-world confirmation
suite. Binary AUROC and regression RMSE are primary; multiclass log loss is primary.
Brier loss is additionally central to the mechanism analysis. Bootstrap intervals
over fixed OOF rows omit training instability, and unadjusted candidate screens
omit multiplicity. These limitations prohibit interpreting them as simultaneous
statistical certification.
Shared candidate-search cost is charged to every standalone selector. Capacity
and inference measurements accompany accuracy; matched-budget claims require
dedicated experiments. Paired task-level analyses average folds and seeds before
resampling tasks. No row-level significance claims across tasks are permitted.

\subsection{Real Development Panel}
Experiment 004 uses the complete sklearn-bundled Wisconsin diagnostic breast
cancer and wine datasets \citep{wdbc1993,wine1992}, and diabetes regression
\citep{efron2004}. They contain respectively 569/30, 178/13, and 442/10
rows/features. All features are numerical and no entries are missing. These
three tasks were named before scored results, to exercise binary, multiclass,
and regression evaluation. They are development tasks, not a representative
confirmation suite. IID row splitting is assumed; the bundled feature tables
supply no grouping/time identifiers to evaluate that assumption.

The diabetes loader explicitly uses \texttt{scaled=False}; its default global
centering/scaling would precede the evaluation splits. Any subsequent learned
transform is fitted on training rows. The positive breast-cancer class is
sklearn's benign class, so its AUPRC describes that convention. Wine targets are
encoded 0/1/2. Dataset versions, loader arguments, source attribution, raw data,
content hashes, and target conventions accompany every run. The UCI sources
state CC-BY-4.0; the diabetes source's separate dataset license remains unspecified.

Two fixed configurations compare feature fractions .1 and .5 at $B=100$, $K=8$,
depth 3 and minimum leaf size 5. With ceiling rounding, these produce 3/2/1 versus
15/7/5 features per learner across the three tasks. Seeds 11/29 and three outer
and three OOF folds give six repeated evaluations per task/width; the same raw
dataset is reused across seeds. Width changes the candidate population rather
than generating nested feature masks. Unchanged baselines and outer/inner
partitions must agree exactly across the two runs. Forest/boosting configurations
are fixed, and a ridge ($\alpha=1$) or logistic ($C=1$) control uses fold-local
standardization. No hyperparameter tuning is performed. Primary metrics remain
task-specific; no pooled ranking or task-population interval is reported for
this panel with one dataset per task type. Split effects are descriptive and
their overlapping observations are not independent uncertainty replicates.

\subsection{Larger Development Sweep (Registered Experiment 006)}
A predeclared follow-up uses credit default, the OpenML 23512 version-2 HIGGS
subset, MiniBooNE, superconductivity, and California housing. Each is capped
at 20,000 rows with fixed seed 7321, stratified for binary classification.
Feature counts are 23, 28, 50, 81, and 8 respectively. The requested harder
classification emphasis motivates the panel; observed baseline AUC is not an
inclusion filter. California supplies a larger low-dimensional regression control.
Exact repeated raw feature vectors are grouped in both outer and OOF folds.
This controls duplicate-row leakage, particularly in superconductivity, but
supplies neither geographic nor unseen-composition extrapolation evidence.
Public-source hashes, original row IDs, categorical conventions, source-count
mismatches, licenses, and every scope restriction accompany the protocol.

Each outer fold generates 6,000 fixed-mask trees, balanced across fractions
$\{.05,.20,.50\}$ and depths $\{3,6\}$, minimum leaf size 10. Prefix pools
$B=1,000/3,000/6,000$ are crossed independently with $K=16/64/128$ for random,
quality, and co-error selection. Six homogeneous cells each have exactly
$B=1,000,K=64$; additional controls include screening, correlation objectives,
with-replacement selection, and one swap. The unscreened $B=6,000,K=64$ co-error
setting is the predesignated anchor. One seed, three outer folds and two OOF
folds are exploratory; no best outer-test setting is presented as a tuned model.
Cluster-bootstrap multiplicities and matrix-free selector columns accelerate
computations while matching literal-resampling and dense toy references.
All settings are charged the complete shared-library search cost, a conservative
accounting convention rather than a matched independent-training budget.
The registered plan and smoke audit precede main scoring; results are pending.
